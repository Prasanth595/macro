Is this scheduling an activity at a time? Opening schedule/calendar/create an event/add to my calendar is a strong YES. '7pm monday dinner at cava', 'mon 7pm dinner at cava' and timed calls are YES without a prefix. A deliverable with a deadline is a task. Opening message/email/task/note/search/find/ask strongly signals another type; meeting requests and dates inside an outgoing body do not create an event.
